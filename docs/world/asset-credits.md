# Open world asset credits

Every third-party asset used in the open world (sub-project #3), with its licence. `Scripts/world/fetch_assets.py` downloads the packs into `SourceArt/World/` (gitignored). Only the imported UE assets under `Content/Shinobi/World/Library` are committed.

**Original content (this project, no third-party licence):**
- Japanese architecture and camp pieces: houses, round house, shrine, torii, main gate, wall segments, stall, lanterns, well, training post, tent, palisade, campfire, bridge. Generated by `Scripts/world/blender/make_gap_pieces.py`; original designs.
- Terrain: heightmap and weightmaps from `Scripts/world/make_heightmap.py`.
- Placeholder sounds: `Scripts/make_sfx.py`.
